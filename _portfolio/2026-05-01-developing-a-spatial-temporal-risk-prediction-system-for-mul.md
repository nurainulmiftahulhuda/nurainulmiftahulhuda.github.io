### Project Description

Developing a Spatial-Temporal Risk Prediction System for Multiple Environmental Hazards Using Satellite Imagery and IoT. This project was conducted as part of ongoing research in spatio-temporal statistics and environmental risk modelling, with Nur'ainul Miftahul Huda serving as Principal Investigator.
